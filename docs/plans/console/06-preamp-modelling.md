# 06 — Preamp modelling

> **Partly settled by Adam's mockup, 2026-09-09 --
> [`THE-STRIP.md`](THE-STRIP.md).** It is not a device at the head of a chain:
> it is the strip's own input stage, `pre in / drive`, drawn at the top beside
> the **Moo / Grip / Punch / Iron** selector that governs the whole strip.
> That makes it part of step 03's one face rather than a separate later build,
> so this step is now about the *modelling* -- what each voicing's input stage
> actually does -- rather than about where the control goes.
>
> And it has a target rather than an adjective. `THE-STRIP.md` records the
> method: author the shaper through its Chebyshev decomposition so each
> voicing's harmonic amplitudes at the operating level are specified and
> tested, and give `Iron` the frequency-dependent drive that is most of why a
> transformer sounds like one on a kick and nearly clean on a hat.

Deliberately last, and deliberately not designed here.

What is settled: it is a device, and it goes at the head of a strip. Nothing
else is, and it should be planned after living with step 02 and step 03 --
because those two are what will say whether the character this plan is after
comes from the summing, from the strip, or from a stage in front of both.

Writing the design now would be guessing at that answer before the two things
that can settle it exist.
