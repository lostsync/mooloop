## Findings, ranked by impact

### 1. Opening an automation lane mallocs on the audio thread, and three paths free one there — MEDIUM/HIGH

`crates/mooloop-core/src/automation.rs:9-10` says "lane edits are applied on
the audio thread and must not allocate", and `pattern.rs:165-167` says
`open_lane` "never reallocates on the audio thread". Both are true of the
outer `lanes` vector (`ChannelPattern::new`, `pattern.rs:103`, reserved to
`MAX_AUTOMATION_LANES_PER_CHANNEL`) and false of what it holds:
`open_lane` (`pattern.rs:168-177`) pushes `AutomationLane::new(target)`,
whose body is `Vec::with_capacity(MAX_AUTOMATION_POINTS_PER_LANE)`
(`automation.rs:68-74`; 1024 points, 12 KB). The chain from the callback is
`Executor::process_with_input` → `apply_command` →
`EngineCommand::OpenAutomationLane` (`render.rs:4876-4883`) →
`Sequencer::open_automation_lane` (`sequencer.rs:270-280`) → `open_lane`;
`UpsertAutomationPoint` (`render.rs:4908`, `sequencer.rs:310-320`) opens
the lane the same way, so the first breakpoint drawn on a destination
allocates too. The session sends both live
(`crates/mooloop-session/src/automation.rs:66,159,188`).

The frees are the same object from the other end. `RemoveAutomationLane`
(`render.rs:4884-4895` → `sequencer.rs:281-291` → `pattern.rs:179-182`,
`lanes.remove(index)`, dropped at the `.is_some()`);
`StructuralCommand::RemoveEffect` → `forget_device` (`render.rs:3894`) →
`structure.rs:759` `lanes.retain(..)`, which the comment at
`pattern.rs:184-186` calls "without allocating" and which frees instead;
and `StructuralCommand::AddChannel` (`render.rs:4369`) →
`Sequencer::clear_channel` (`sequencer.rs:171-178`), which walks
`&mut self.patterns` -- the whole preallocated bank, `MAX_PATTERNS = 256`
(`channel.rs:13`), not `active_patterns` as `forget_device` beside it is
bounded (`sequencer.rs:344-346`) -- and `lanes.clear()`s each. Up to
256 × 8 frees on Add Channel, and 256 iterations of nothing in the usual
case. `ClearAutomationLane` (`automation.rs:170-172`, `points.clear()`)
keeps its capacity and is clean, which shows the intent.

No allocation-counting test measures any of these: the three in
`executor.rs` (`:691`, `:713`, `:799`) cover install, the carry plan and the
routing tables. `reserve_points` (`automation.rs:106-112`) exists for the
decode path, so the inner vector's capacity was thought about, on the one
path that runs off the thread. Plan A.

### 2. A loop fold now zeroes every delay, reverb and plate ring in the project, every lap — MEDIUM, and a question

`render.rs:5683-5695` sets `jumped` from `span.jumped`, which
`transport.rs:154-155` reserves for a loop fold; `:5708-5710` then sends
`on_discontinuity(Discontinuity::Seek)` to every live strip's source and
chain and every bus chain (`:4536-4543`, `:1571-1577` including bypassed
and sleeping slots). Delay (`effects/delay.rs:221-229` →
`delayline.rs:56-60`, `fill(0.0)` on a two-second stereo ring: 768 KB at
48 kHz), reverb (`reverb.rs:603-615`, eight lines, four diffusers, a
predelay) and plate (`plate.rs:395`) all clear on `Seek`. That landed in
`6e76c56`, after yesterday's tree, and is deliberate: the plan says the hook
is "said by the engine at a seek, a loop fold, a stop". Two things it did
not weigh.

The cost scales with the project and not the block: a fold is every few
seconds in a loop-based instrument, and each one is a memset of every
tail-holding ring in the song inside one callback, on top of the voice
release that was already there (`:5687`). Nothing measures it;
`block_cost.rs` has no fold case.

And the behaviour is the one a groove box is least expected to have. Voices
were already released at the fold, so the delay's *input* stops; clearing
the *line* means no repeat and no reverb tail ever crosses the loop point,
which is audible on every lap of every song with a delay in it. Whether a
fold is a seek for a tail is Adam's call, and `Discontinuity` has no variant
that lets a node tell them apart (`node.rs:150-164`: `Seek`, `Stop`,
`ProgramChange`), so today the delay cannot decline the fold without also
declining the seek. Plan C makes it expressible; the default stays his.

### 5. Three bounded costs on the callback that grow with the song, not the block — LOW/MEDIUM

- **Song-mode automation lookup.** Once `has_automation_at`
  (`sequencer.rs:477-501`) is true, `curve_for` (`render.rs:780-794`) is
  asked once per source descriptor per channel (`:5903-5905`), per internal
  route (`:5956`), per effect descriptor per slot (`:1360`) and twice per
  strip (`:2024`), and each call is `automation_lane_at` (`sequencer.rs:526-574`),
  which in Song mode walks the whole playlist (`:545`, up to

  `active_channels × 8` lanes. It is zero until the first lane exists under

- **`EventList::push_ordered`** (`event.rs:122-133`) is an insertion sort

- **`Sequencer::set_playlist_placement`** (`sequencer.rs:123-144`) does

Also on the thread and noted, not ranked: `defer_command`'s

## Architecture

Checked against the code, not the plans' claims.

**The automation store is the next thing that fights automation.** Lanes are
`Vec<AutomationLane>` inside `Vec<ChannelPattern>` inside a 256-pattern bank,
with a `Vec<AutomationPoint>` inside each lane; the outer two are reserved
and the inner one is minted on the callback (finding 1). Song-level
automation -- Adam's decision 1 expects long-form audio to arrive through it
-- has nowhere to live: every resolver is pattern-scoped
(`automation_lane_at` `sequencer.rs:526`, `has_automation_at` `:477`,
`pattern_lane_destination` `:450`, `restore_lanes_left_behind`
`render.rs:4074`), and the Song-mode lookup is already a walk of the
playlist per destination per block (finding 5). Recording a knob as a lane
is the fourth writer the precedence table names and does not have
(`render.rs:1314-1315`, `MODULATION.md:257-260`); it will write points from
the audio thread at control rate, so it needs the lane bank to be fixed
storage before it exists, not after. Plan A is the cheap first move: a
lane bank whose points are allocated with the bank, opened and closed by
flipping a target, makes the record-writer's storage problem disappear and
gives song-level lanes a shape to copy.

**The discontinuity hook has one kind too few.** `Discontinuity::Seek` is
sent for a seek and for a loop fold (`render.rs:5708-5710`), and a node that
wants to keep its tail across the fold cannot say so (finding 2). A fourth
variant costs one enum arm and one line in each of four `on_discontinuity`
impls; it is the difference between a rule the nodes can express and one
the renderer decides for them. The voice path still uses `Event::Choke` for
a seek, as step 03 recorded, and ML-P8 shows what happens when a device
contains another device: the hook stops at the outer one (finding 4).
`plugin-hosting` should read finding 4 as the shape of its own problem --
a hosted plugin is a device inside a device, and every contract the strip
forwards (`on_discontinuity`, `skip_block`, `is_at_rest`, latency) has to
be forwarded again inside the slot.

**The parameter address space is closed, and every writer bottoms out on
it.** `ParamAddr { scope, owner, param }` (`modulation.rs:63-70`) is what
lanes, routes, MIDI bindings and knob commands all resolve to, and
`param` means something only through `EffectKind::descriptors()`
(`effect.rs:128-145`), a `&'static` table per variant. A plugin with runtime
ids has no representation; `SCOPE.md:386-403` and the plugin plan say so
and nothing has landed on it. The one-time decision -- a `ParamOwner`
variant for an instance-scoped sparse id, or `param: u32` reinterpreted per
owner -- should be made before automation, modulation and binding
persistence (all keyed on `ParamAddr`/`ParamKey`) multiply further. Not a
Sonnet-alone plan.

**MOO-56 re-verified at HEAD, and one asymmetry worth recording.**
`ChannelStrip` has eight concrete fields (`render.rs:2289-2297`) and three
exhaustive matches (`source_node` `:2504`, `source_and_bus` `:2528`,
`choke_group` `:2608`); the session has two matches (`session.rs:465-472`,
`:501-536`) over eight per-kind fields (`session/channel.rs:43-50`); the UI
has `device_kind_to_int` and the picker array (`lib.rs:2308,2347`).
`mooloop-project` has **no** exhaustive arm on `DeviceKind` at all: serde's
tagged envelope and three `let ChannelSource::Sampler(..) = .. else` guards
(`project/src/lib.rs:616,892,1227`). Persistence is already additive-safe;
engine, session and core are not.

**Recording and quit.** Finding 3 is a day older and no cheaper. The next
person to touch `take.rs` should land yesterday's Plan B before anything
else in it.

**Undo is two nets.** Dirty is generic (`engine.rs:701`) and therefore
complete, to the point of counting an arm as an edit (finding 6). History
is per handler and therefore has whatever holes nobody has walked into yet
(finding 7). A `gesture` helper that takes a snapshot, runs a closure and
records is the shape the eleven console verbs and the four input picks
need; `LOOSE_ENDS.md:504-506` has already proposed it.
