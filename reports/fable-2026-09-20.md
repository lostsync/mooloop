Yesterday's finding 2 was a note-on-sized window. Two things widened it
since:

- `RenderState::advance_takes` (`crates/mooloop-engine/src/render.rs:6070`)
  takes `self.audio_input_routing.load()` and holds the guard across the
  whole per-channel loop (`:6073-6097`) on every block in which any strip
  has a live take. It is called from `process_block` (`:6037`) under
  `Executor::process_with_input` (`executor.rs:296`) under the JACK callback
  (`jack_driver.rs:68`).
- `monitors_input` (`render.rs:4984-4988`) takes a fresh guard for *each*
  live channel on *each* block, whether or not anything is recording: the
  render loop asks it at `:5718` to decide whether the channel may sleep.
  That landed in `b9bb4d8`, after yesterday's tree.

callback. The session sends that store on every audio-input pick
(`crates/mooloop-session/src/engine.rs:715`).
