## Findings, ranked by impact

### 1. The `ArcSwap` window is now open every block, on every live channel, and its fix was applied and reverted — MEDIUM (carried over, re-ranked)

Yesterday's finding 2 was a note-on-sized window. Two things widened it
since:

- `RenderState::advance_takes` (`crates/mooloop-engine/src/render.rs:6070`)
  takes `self.audio_input_routing.load()` and holds the guard across the
  whole per-channel loop (`:6073-6097`) on every block in which any strip
  has a live take. It is called from `process_block` (`:6037`) under
  `Executor::process_with_input` (`executor.rs:296`) under the JACK callback
  (`jack_driver.rs:68`).
- `monitors_input` (`render.rs:4984-4988`) takes a fresh guard for *each*
  live channel on *each* block, whether or not anything is recording: the
  render loop asks it at `:5718` to decide whether the channel may sleep.
  That landed in `b9bb4d8`, after yesterday's tree.

The writer is unchanged: `SharedCells::set_audio_input_routing`
(`lib.rs:651-654`) does `store(Arc::new(AudioInputRouting { taps }))` and
keeps nothing, so a guard live at the store is the last owner of a
`Vec<Option<AudioTap>>` (`render.rs:2794-2796`) and frees it in the
callback. The session sends that store on every audio-input pick
(`crates/mooloop-session/src/engine.rs:715`).

`db02366` fixed this exactly as yesterday's Plan B described (three boxes,
three structural commands, displaced box out through the reclaim ring, an
allocation test, the rule written into `AUDIO_ARCHITECTURE.md`), passed
rung 4 on the box, and `eb4f605` reverted it to merge past `7cd61f0`,
stating "deferred, not abandoned". A trial `git cherry-pick -n db02366`
onto `19c467f` (in a throwaway worktree, discarded) conflicts in eleven
hunks: seven in `executor.rs` (the install arm and its tests, where
`7d4cf34` did the same finding-1 work differently), two in `lib.rs:375,476`
(the reclaim enum and `PreparedProject`) and two in `render.rs:3015,3176`
(the `RenderState` fields and constructor). Every one is the finding-1 half
colliding with its twin; the finding-2 half applies clean. Plan A is
therefore "take `db02366`'s finding-2 half by hand", not a cherry-pick.

### 2. A take in flight has no owner at three edges: quit, a failed drain, and a channel that changed kind — MEDIUM

The capture path is clean on the audio thread (below) and careful about
every edge the plan named. Three it did not name:

- **Quit.** `on_quit_requested` (`crates/mooloop-ui/src/lib.rs:5675-5687`)
  and `on_close_requested` (`:6238-6247`) ask about `session.dirty` and
  nothing else. A live take is not dirty. The drain is a detached
  `JoinHandle` (`crates/mooloop-session/src/take.rs:82,147`) that
  `TakeRecorder` never joins on drop (no `Drop` impl in the file); the
  WAV's `finalize()` runs only at the end of `drain` (`:385`). Quit during a
  take ends the process with the header unpatched, and the take is gone
  with no dialog.
- **A failed drain keeps its file.** `drain` removes the file only on
  `Drained::Empty` (`take.rs:388-390`); a `Failed` write or finalize
  (`:353-357,385-387`) returns with the partial file in `recordings/`,
  where step 06 ("find and delete takes nothing refers to") is not started
  (`audio-recording/00-status.md`). The pump logs it (`lib.rs:13527`).
- **The channel is no longer a sampler when the take lands.** `apply_take`
  finds the channel by id (`lib.rs:15002-15013`) and calls
  `apply_loaded_sample` (`:14907-14957`), which checks no kind: it sends
  `set_channel_audio` to the engine slot and writes `sample_name`,
  `sample_data`, `waveform` and the rest onto a channel that
  `reset_channel_source` (`crates/mooloop-session/src/session.rs:432,474-493`)
  cleared when the kind changed. The result is a sample the face cannot show
  and the save cannot write (the `Sampler`-only arm at `session.rs:500-517`),
  a "Record Take" undo entry, and `dirty` for nothing. The channel-gone
  case has the right answer one branch up; this case needs the same one.

One line more of the same shape: `record_press`
(`crates/mooloop-session/src/take.rs:240-256`) arms whenever
`audio_input` is not `Off`, and a source the picker reports `is_missing`
(`crates/mooloop-core/src/input.rs:188`, drawn at `lib.rs:5094`) is not
`Off`. REC on a channel whose source was deleted records a silent take with
no message. Plan B.

### 3. The RECORD page's phase is spelled in Rust and in Slint, and nothing reads both — LOW

`TakeStatus` (`crates/mooloop-engine/src/take.rs:45-50`, atomics) is the
truth; `TakeView` (`crates/mooloop-session/src/take.rs:87-92,165-176`) is a
pass-through. The third copy is the window's `sampler_record_state`, which
`publish_take` (`crates/mooloop-ui/src/lib.rs:5112-5146`) encodes as
`Waiting → 1`, recording `→ 2`, else `0`, and which
`sampler-device.slint:1082-1139` decodes with a ternary ladder
(`record-state == 2 ? "STOP" : record-state == 1 ? "WAIT" : "REC"`, `:1086`).
`record-max-bars: 64` (`:293`) is a second spelling of `MAX_RECORD_BARS`
(`crates/mooloop-core/src/input.rs:208`); Rust overwrites it at `lib.rs:5107`,
so it is a dead default today. `slint_face_agreement.rs` reads
`sampler-device.slint` for the sampler's descriptors and not for the
record page; no test in `crates/mooloop-ui/tests` names `record-state` or
`record-max-bars`. The CLIP and LENGTH toggles already needed a write-back
(`lib.rs:9086-9101`, "a click sets but nothing else does") to keep the
window's copy honest, which is the same fault one level down. This is the
codebase's characteristic fault (`AGENTS.md`, "Duplication") on a page that
is three days old. Plan C.
